HOW TO PLAY

An outbreak is about to hit the city. You get one planning phase before it starts.

1. Draw a safe zone. Click its corners, then click the first corner (or press Enter) to close it. Bigger zones cost more. Civilians run for it once the outbreak starts.

2. Gates and walls. Every street that crosses your zone's edge starts as a gate (blue): civilians can get in, infected can't, but gates break sooner. Click one to make it a wall (white): stronger, but nobody gets in. During the battle you can still close gates, and you should once everyone is inside. Walls also stop your own ground units: tanks, APCs and squads only go in and out through gates or breaches, and turrets, tanks, APCs, squads and the gunboat can't shoot through a wall or the buildings on your zone's edge. Mortars, howitzers, HIMARS, the battleship, rooftop units and aircraft fire over them. Units shut outside can still be rearmed by supply drops and cargo drones.

3. Units. Garrisons and Snipers go on buildings, Turrets on streets. Taller buildings give snipers more range (press V to see heights). Units inside the zone never run out of ammo; outside, they do. Hover over any button or placed unit to inspect its full tactical stats in the Info Panel.

4. Warning. When the battle starts an emergency broadcast goes out: people head for your safe zones and the first cases turn somewhere outside your zones after 20 seconds.

5. Evacuate. Build Evac Points (rooftop helipads) inside your zone: every 12 s a lift flies people out, and trains carry people out on the rails. Each extra pad costs double the last ($3M, $6M, $12M...), so plan where they go. Each person flown out brings $24,000 in federal evacuation funding. A lift waves off if infected crowd the pad.

Money is real. Prices are real-world US dollars: ground hardware and helicopters at their unit price, ships and jets at the cost of the mission. Ordnance is cheap next to hardware but limited per battle, like a real stockpile (one MOAB, one nuke, four Tomahawks). The end card shows what you spent and the property damage bill.

6. Survive the hordes. Hordes walk in from outside the city along highways, rail lines, bridges and the river (an amber arrow shows where the next one comes in). Each is bigger and comes sooner. The round ends when nobody is left in the city (everyone alive is out), or when only a few stuck stragglers remain. Your round score (0 to 100) is mostly people saved, plus how fast, how intact the city is and how many of the infected you killed: Bronze 10, Silver 30, Gold 50, Platinum 75 points, and at least 5% saved for any medal. Then Keep fighting: endless, harder hordes scored as Mayhem (kills plus city damage, the more the better) until you pull out. Bonus XP for keeping the city intact (Light Touch) or for wrecking it (Scorched Earth, 35% wrecked or a $1 billion damage bill). Pause menu: Pull out ends the battle. End round (top bar, after the outbreak) scores the round right away when only stragglers are left: people in the zone count as saved, the rest are left behind.

Abilities & Realistic Deployment. Buy abilities while planning or mid-battle: Airstrike sends an F-15E Strike Eagle to bomb a line, Firebomb drops incendiary canisters, Paratroopers sends a C-130J Hercules to drop an airborne squad on parachutes, and Supply drop parachutes 4 GPS-guided crates that refill 100% ammo, heal mobile squads, repair walls within 70 m, and leave a field supply depot for 45 s. Mid-battle units arrive via UH-60 Black Hawk, CH-47 Chinook sling-load, or C-130J heavy APC parachute drop, and Supply Depots dispatch autonomous Cargo Drones.

Terrain & Structure Destruction. Heavy weapons (Mortars, Howitzers, Battleships, Airstrikes, and Fuel Depots) crater streets, scorch terrain, shear floors off buildings, and collapse heavily shelled buildings into walkable rubble that slows advancing hordes by 30%.

Money. Every 10 seconds of battle you earn money for each person safe in your zones, plus a little for every infected killed. Spend it any time: new units (they take a few seconds to set up), abilities, bridge charges, even extra safe zones (those cost a lot more). Gates can be opened and closed whenever you like. Stronger units take longer to set up. Selling a unit mid-battle (Sell / Evac) dispatches an extraction helicopter to pull it out; then you get its cost back.

More units. The Mortar and Howitzer shell crowds from far away. The APC, the Gunboat (on the river) and Paratroopers think for themselves: they hunt crowds near where you put them, go after infected chasing civilians first, back off before they get swarmed, and go home to reload. APCs also dismount a Mechanized Rifle Team on first contact. The Battleship shells crowds far inland from the river. Click any unit with Move / Aim to change what it shoots first: nearest, closest to the walls, or infected chasing civilians.

Support buildings. Built on buildings inside your safe zone: Budget office cuts the cost of nearby units and buildings by 20%, Repair yard mends damaged walls, gates and vehicles, Radio tower extends nearby firing range by 40%, Supply depot feeds ammo via cargo drones to turrets, mobile squads and forward units outside the walls (and halves vehicle reload time), Command post boosts firing rate by 30% and patrol range by 25%, Precinct dispatches autonomous Police Squads, and Armory arms Militia squads to guard the walls while reinforcing indoor barricades.

Real Cities, Mutators, Daily Challenge & Kill Heatmaps. Play on 8 built-in real OpenStreetMap cities (Castle Rock, Denver, Chicago, Pittsburgh, Boston, Manhattan, Portland, Paris) or search any real city live when online. Toggle Mutators on the Cities screen or play the deterministic Daily Challenge. Press H (or hover over any unit button in battle) to view spatial Kill Heatmaps showing where your defenses did the most damage.

Controls. Right-drag or WASD to move the map, wheel to zoom, Space to pause, 1/2/3 for speed, Q recalls units to the zone (again: back to their posts), L locks gates, K clears the zone, Z X C V B N M use quick bar slots 1-7, J height view, R shows unit ranges, H toggles kill heatmap, Esc opens the menu, F5 quick save (the battle goes in your slot), F11 fullscreen (Windows app).
